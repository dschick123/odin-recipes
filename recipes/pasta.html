Garlic Butter Pasta

Description

This garlic butter pasta is a quick, comforting meal made with just a few simple ingredients you probably already have in your kitchen. Tender noodles are tossed in a rich sauce of melted butter, golden garlic, and freshly grated Parmesan cheese.

It comes together in about 15 minutes, making it perfect for busy weeknights. Serve it on its own or pair it with grilled chicken, shrimp, or a fresh side salad.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces spaghetti
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  • Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until tender, then drain
  • Melt the butter in a pan and cook the garlic for 1 to 2 minutes
  • Add the spaghetti to the pan and toss it in the garlic butter
  • Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and serve warm
