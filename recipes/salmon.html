Lemon Baked Salmon

Description

This lemon baked salmon is a light, healthy dinner that's full of fresh flavor. The salmon bakes in the oven with lemon slices and a drizzle of olive oil, coming out tender, flaky, and perfectly seasoned.

It takes only a few minutes to prepare and about 15 minutes to cook. Serve it with rice, roasted vegetables, or a simple green salad for a balanced meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F
  2. Place the salmon on a baking sheet and drizzle with olive oil
  3. Season with salt and pepper and top with lemon slices
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the salmon flakes easily
